Controls

When the automatic pass isn't enough, you get the dials.

Most files need nothing. For the ones that do, the detector is not a black box — every threshold it uses is in the panel, with the defaults it shipped with.

Mask size

Grows the treated area when a stamp sits slightly outside where it was found. Left at 1.00× it treats exactly the detected region.

Gain when the stamp drifts

How hard to push when the mark is not where it usually sits. Lower it if a correction is leaving a soft patch behind.

Accept and trust thresholds

The match scores that decide whether a watermark is really there. Lower the accept threshold to catch fainter marks.

Scan stride

How finely to search when the stamp has moved. Smaller is more thorough and slower; 8 pixels is the balance it ships with.

Questions

The things worth knowing first.

Do my images get uploaded anywhere?

No. Decoding, detection and reconstruction all happen in your browser. The only things that travel to us are your sign-in and your credit balance.

What counts as a credit?

One cleaned file. A free account gets 15 a day, refreshed at midnight UTC. Nothing is deducted when a file turns out to have no watermark on it.

Which video resolutions work?

720p and 1080p MP4. Upscaled 4K exports are not supported yet — the mark lands differently after Flow's upsampler, and a bad correction is worse than none, so the panel tells you instead of guessing.

Google Flow now has its own watermark toggle. Do I still need this?

If your Flow account has the built-in option, use it — the extension will offer to stand down for Flow and keep working on Gemini and AI Studio. Running both is just extra processing.

Can I use it on files I didn't make in Google's tools?

Yes. Drop any PNG, JPG, WebP or MP4 into the side panel. The detector is tuned for Google's sparkle, so results on other marks vary.

What happens to my credits if I cancel?

You return to the free tier and keep getting 15 a day. Nothing you have already cleaned is affected.
