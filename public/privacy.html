Erasezo.com

Privacy

Last updated 21 September 2026

Removal runs on your machine. erasezo.com sees your account and credit counts, not the files you clean.

What is processed locally

Images and video are decoded, detected and reconstructed in your browser by the Erasezo extension. There is no upload host in that path. A copy of your file is not stored on erasezo.com, because it never arrives.

What erasezo.com receives

The site and API only exchange what an account needs to exist and be metered:

  • Sign-in: email, a password hash or a Google account id, and a session.
  • Credit counts: daily allowance, remaining balance, and a ledger of spends and grants.
  • Plan and subscription status, once a paid plan is attached to the account.

That is the traffic to erasezo.com. Prompts, pixels and video frames are not in it.

Cookies and local storage

  • erasezo_token — the signed-in session. HttpOnly, SameSite=Lax. Set on login, cleared on logout.
  • erasezo_admin — a separate cookie for the Control Room, minted only after password and TOTP. The ordinary session cookie does not open it.
  • Theme preference is stored in this browser’s local storage, not as a cookie, and is never sent to the server.

There is no analytics pixel and no advertising cookie.

Payments

When checkout is enabled, paid plans are billed by Razorpay. Card numbers go to Razorpay, not to us. We keep the resulting subscription status and the credit allowance that follows from it.

Until Razorpay keys are configured, paid checkout is not offered. The free plan does not go through a payment processor.

Contact

Account questions are handled from the dashboard once you are signed in. There is no mailer, so a forgotten password cannot be reset by email — use Google sign-in if the address is a Google account, or stay signed in.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of the page changes with it. The live page at /privacy is the current version.
